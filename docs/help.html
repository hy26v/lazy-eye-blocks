Color setup

Set the colors for each eye

With anaglyph glasses on, tune each eye color until its test block disappears through the opposite lens but stays visible through the intended lens. The black preview matches the game background.

  1. Open the game and choose Settings, then Left eye color.
  2. Look through your right lens and adjust the color until the test block blends into the black background. Check that it remains visible through your left lens.
  3. Repeat for Right eye color, using your left lens to hide the block and your right lens to see it.

Color perception and glasses vary, so adjust the colors in your own setup.

Tile mix

Choose how colors are assigned

Color by shapes gives each new shape one eye color. Color by cells assigns a color to each cell independently.

In Red / blue tile mix, adjust the chance for each channel in 5% steps. The red and blue shares always add up to 100%.

Playfield

Adjust the board and speed

  • Board size changes rows and columns. The board resizes to fit the available screen.
  • Speed level changes how quickly pieces fall. You can lock the level to keep the pace steady.
  • Settings can be changed between games or from the in-game toolbar.

During a run

Read your stats

Points earned. Clearing several rows at once earns more points.

The total number of full rows removed from the board.
Level
The current fall speed setting.
Next piece
A preview of the piece that will enter play after the current one lands.

Your browser

Settings are saved automatically

Colors, tile mix, board size, and speed settings stay in this browser between visits. Use Save settings and Load settings in the Settings menu to manage a saved copy manually.
